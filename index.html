What should I do next — and is an old quest holding me back?

Questie Compass reads the Questie data already on your computer, follows every prerequisite chain, and shows the low-level quests you skipped that are now blocking the content you actually want. Nothing is uploaded; everything runs in this tab.

  1. Log out of WoW or type /reload so Questie writes its latest data.
  2. Choose your WoW folder below — the _classic_era_ folder (or the World of Warcraft folder above it).
  3. Pick your character, confirm level and race, and explore.
or

Usually C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_classic_era_ — browsers won't let a page pick where the dialog opens, but Windows remembers your last choice. Your browser will ask to confirm “uploading” the folder — that is just how it grants this page read access; nothing leaves your computer. Picking only the WTF folder works too (you lose the installed-Questie and RestedXP extras).
